Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Pace Running Pace Calculator does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

The app has no analytics, no tracking, no advertisements, and makes no network requests. Your theme preference is stored locally on your device and never leaves it.

When you share a pace band, the image is handed to the iOS share sheet and goes only where you send it (a printer, Photos, AirDrop, or another app you choose).

If you have any questions, please get in touch via the App Store.
